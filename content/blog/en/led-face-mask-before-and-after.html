What can you really expect from an LED face mask, in pictures? Rather than photos of models, here are our testers: real people, photographed before and after several weeks of their NŪR Protocol routine. Above all, here is how to read them with a fair eye, ours as well as any other brand’s.

How these photos were taken

Each pair shows one and the same person, photographed under the same light and from the same angle, before the first session and at the end of their testing period. No photo is retouched beyond standard exposure correction, and every tester gave written consent to publication.

No tester followed their routine for less than seven weeks, with four to five sessions a week. That matters, because an LED mask does not transform the skin in a few days. Published studies on LED light cover similar periods, usually four to ten weeks.

What these photos show, and what they do not

These photos show individual results, achieved by people who followed their routine consistently for at least seven weeks. They are not a promise. Every skin reacts in its own way, and the result depends on its starting point, on the routine followed, on how regular the sessions are, and on everything else that changes along the way, from sun exposure to the skincare in use.

Nor do they replace a clinical study. What research documents best is the effect of red light on texture and fine lines, and of blue light on blemishes in mild to moderate acne, as explained in our article on LED face mask benefits. For the rest, we would rather show honest photos than unverifiable percentages.

Why consistency matters more than anything

What these testers have in common is not a miracle setting, it is consistency. Four to five sessions a week, week after week, with the right light at the right time. That is precisely the role of NŪR Skincoach, the app that comes with NŪR Protocol. It assesses your skin with a short questionnaire, and a selfie if you choose, and that assessment gives your personalised routine, so you know which light to use, in which order and how often, without guesswork.

The user manual recommends three to five sessions a week, in four-week cycles, and going beyond that does not speed anything up. The neck deserves the same attention as the face, as we explain in our article on the LED face and neck mask.

How to take your own before and after photos

To judge your own results fairly, reproduce our testers’ conditions. Photograph your face and neck before the first session, by the same window, at the same time of day, without make-up or filter, and with your head in exactly the same position. Repeat every two weeks in the same conditions, then compare the photos side by side rather than from memory. Note anything that changes in your routine along the way, since a new serum or a sunny holiday can change your skin too.

FAQ

How long does an LED face mask take to show results?

Our testers followed their routine for at least seven weeks, with four to five sessions a week, and published studies usually last four to ten weeks. Every skin has its own pace, and no timeline can be guaranteed.

Are the before and after photos retouched?

No. They only receive standard exposure correction, and each pair is taken under the same light and from the same angle, with the tester’s written consent.

Are the results guaranteed?

No. These photos show individual results. The outcome depends on the skin, the routine followed and how regular the sessions are.

How many sessions a week should I do?

The NŪR Protocol user manual recommends three to five sessions a week, in four-week cycles, and NŪR Skincoach adjusts the frequency in your personalised routine.
